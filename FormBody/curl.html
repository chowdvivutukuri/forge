Arm long

Target muscles

  • PrimaryBiceps brachiiFront of the upper arm. Bends the elbow and turns the palm up.
  • SecondaryBrachialisUnder the biceps, just above the elbow. Pure elbow flexor.
  • SecondaryBrachioradialisThumb side of the forearm near the elbow. Helps most mid-range.

Range of motion

Start with the arm almost straight and finish with the forearm well past vertical. The upper arm barely moves.

Joint angles, live

    Equipment

    Pair of hex dumbbells, supinated grip (palms up). Feet hip-width.

    Cues

    • Elbows stay pinned by your sides (shoulder under 15°).
    • Squeeze at the top, around 40–45° at the elbow.
    • Lower all the way to about 165°, not locked hard.
    • No hip swing: torso stays upright.
